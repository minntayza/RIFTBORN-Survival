# Zawgyi evolution artwork
Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Final assets are 512 × 512 PNGs in `public/assets/card-concepts/`.

Royal Dat Lone (`zawgyi-royal-evolution-v2.png`): Two equal full gold-and-ruby orbs, separated diagonally with a clear gap between their complete solid shells. Intertwined golden and cream trails, star highlights, crimson backdrop, bold cartoon outlines and clean cel shading. No text, frame, or characters. A repair pass closed an unwanted opening in the initial shells.

Azure Lotus (`zawgyi-azure-evolution-v1.png`): Preserve the original flaming lotus silhouette; change outer petals to deep blue and cyan, flames to cyan and pale cream centers, with a blue-white fire trail and blue embers. Saturated deep-blue background, polished cartoon cel shading, no text or frame.
