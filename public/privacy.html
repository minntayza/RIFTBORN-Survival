Zombie Last Stand — Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Zombie Last Stand offers guest play and optional Google sign-in.

Guest play

The game stores progress and settings in your browser. Clearing site data or changing browsers can remove access to that local progress.

Google sign-in and cloud saves

When you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase process your authentication. Your account can include your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile information supplied by Google. The game uses your account to identify you, display your player name, and associate your saved progress with you. The game does not receive your Google password.

Signed-in progress stored with Supabase includes game records, unlocked characters and cosmetics, in-game currency balances, settings, and saved loadouts. Authentication session data and local save backups are stored in your browser to maintain your session and support progress recovery. Signing out does not delete your cloud save.

Service providers

The game uses Google for sign-in, Supabase for authentication and cloud saves, and Vercel for website hosting. Requests to these services can include technical information such as IP addresses and browser information. Their respective privacy policies explain their handling of service data:

  • Google
  • Supabase
  • Vercel

Your choices and contact

You can keep playing as a guest instead of signing in. You can sign out using the account menu and remove local data using your browser's site-data controls. Removing local browser data does not delete an account or cloud save.

For privacy questions or to request deletion of your game account and its cloud save, contact minntayza16@gmail.com. Account ownership must be confirmed before account data is removed. You can also review or revoke the app's connection through your Google account settings.
